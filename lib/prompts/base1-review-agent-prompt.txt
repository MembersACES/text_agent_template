§ WHEN POLICIES DO NOT APPLY (ELECTRICITY)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Unbundled C&I / SME (energy + network split):**
- Extract **peak / shoulder / off-peak energy rates in c/kWh** from the **retailer energy charge lines only** (printed $/kWh or c/kWh on the invoice). **Do not** derive TOU c/kWh by dividing **(energy + network + other) / kWh** — that blends non-retail components and invalidates comparisons.
- Once energy-only TOU c/kWh are correct, **§B retail comparison (NSW 12/12/10, etc.) applies** the same as for bundled TOU (enforced server-side in Base 1).

**Bundled flat / single-rate / anytime (no TOU split):**
- If the tariff is **flat**, **single rate**, **anytime**, or **all usage is one bucket** (no meaningful shoulder/off split), **do not** apply TOU peak/shoulder/off retail comparisons — server-side Base 1 skips them.

**C&I daily supply:**
- **Do not** include daily retailer “service” / supply charge as a **savings** finding for **C&I** Base 1 reviews (omit from `low_hanging_fruit`).

**Demand (C&I):**
- When **billed/registered demand** aligns with **highest actual metered demand** on the invoice (within normal tolerance), **do not** claim demand **savings**. You may state in prose that demand billing aligns with metered max for this period (no dollar “savings” unless clearly over-billed per § DEMAND REPRICING).


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
§ RETAIL & METERING POLICY (ELECTRICITY — PRECEDENCE OVER LEGACY EXAMPLES)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Applies to TOU retail usage (c/kWh)** for **bundled or unbundled** bills, using **per-period energy-only** rates — **not** flat/single-rate tariffs (see § above).

**A. Identify state for retail TOU**
- From `site_address` (or invoice header): NSW, VIC, ACT, QLD, SA, WA, TAS, NT.
- **NSW** applies NSW retail targets below.
- **VIC, ACT, QLD, SA, WA, TAS, NT** use the **same retail comparison targets as VIC** in §B (i.e. all non-NSW states share the VIC peak/off-peak comparison figures).

**B. Retail TOU comparison rates (c/kWh) — savings target**
These are the **comparison rates** (not the legacy 28–35 c/kWh band). Annual savings for a period = `(current_c/kWh - comparison_c/kWh) / 100 × annual_kWh_for_that_period`. Only include `low_hanging_fruit` if annual savings > **$200** and current **>** comparison.

**NSW (3-period or 2-period where applicable):**
- **Peak** and **Shoulder** use the **same comparison rate: 12 c/kWh** (when the invoice shows peak and shoulder at the **same** effective $/kWh, still use 12 for both periods separately against their respective usage).
- **Off-peak** comparison: **10 c/kWh**.

**All states other than NSW (VIC, ACT, QLD, SA, WA, TAS, NT):**
- **Peak** comparison: **9 c/kWh**.
- **Off-peak** comparison: **7 c/kWh**.
- **Shoulder (3-period only):** use the **peak** comparison rate (**9 c/kWh**), unless shoulder is billed identically to off-peak on the invoice — then use **7 c/kWh** for that shoulder line.

**Severity (retail TOU):**
- **medium**: current rate exceeds the comparison rate for that period, savings > $200/year
- **high**: current rate exceeds comparison by **≥ 5 c/kWh** for that period (or annual savings ≥ $2,000/year)

**Example (NSW peak):** Current peak **15 c/kWh**, comparison **12 c/kWh** → excess **3 c/kWh**; multiply by annual peak kWh for savings message.

**C. Metering (annual) — combine all metering-related charges**
- Include every line item that is **meter charge**, **metering**, **DMA**, and **VAS** (or similar) tied to metering — **sum into one annual metering $ figure** for the site.
- Method: sum eligible charges **ex GST for the bill period**, then `annual_metering = (period_total / billing_days) × 365`.
- Populate **`meter_charges`** in JSON as the **period total** (sum of those lines for the billing period), so downstream annualisation matches.

**Metering verdicts and comparison $ for savings (annualised combined metering):**
- **≤ $700/year:** good price — **do not** add a metering savings finding.
- **> $700 and ≤ $1,200/year:** compare to **$700/year** — potential annual savings = `annual_combined - 700` (only if > $200/year).
- **> $1,200/year:** compare to **$900/year** — potential annual savings = `annual_combined - 900` (only if > $200/year).

**Severity (metering):** medium if in the 700–1,200 band above comparison; high if > 1,200 or savings ≥ $2,000/year.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
§ DEMAND REPRICING (ELECTRICITY)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When **demand charges** exist on the invoice:
1. Extract **`recorded_max_demand_kw`**: the **highest demand (kW or kVA — same unit as billed)** from “highest actual metered demand”, interval max, or equivalent table — take the **maximum** relevant to the charge.
2. Extract **`demand_kw`**: the demand value **actually used for the billed demand charge** (registered/billed kW or **kVA** — store the number in `demand_kw` even when the invoice unit is kVA).
3. Savings apply only if billed demand **materially exceeds** recorded max (typically **≥ 2%** higher than recorded) **and** repricing yields **> $200/year**. Small rounding gaps are **not** savings.
4. If recorded max **≥** billed demand, or values align within tolerance, **no demand savings** — optional prose only: demand billing aligns with highest metered demand this period.

Populate both fields when demand charges exist; null when not applicable.


CRITICAL FIELD REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ELECTRICITY:
- NMI: 10-11 characters (validate)
- peak_usage_kwh, off_peak_usage_kwh: always populated when usage is split
- shoulder_usage_kwh: **null if 2-period TOU (QLD/SA/WA/NT)**
- peak_rate_c_per_kwh, off_peak_rate_c_per_kwh: for **unbundled** TOU, from **energy charge lines only** (c/kWh); for bundled, extract or calculate per guide
- shoulder_rate_c_per_kwh: **null if 2-period TOU**
- daily_supply_charge: in **$/day** — use the retailer **daily / service charge** line (e.g. “Daily Charge” with **$/day**). **Do not** set this to unrelated period totals ÷ billing_days (that inflates $/day). — **do not** use for C&I savings findings
- **demand_kw**: billed/registered demand (**kW or kVA** — same unit as invoice; store numeric value here). Aliases may be normalised from `demand_kva`, `billed_demand_kva`, `maximum_demand_kw`
- **recorded_max_demand_kw**: highest metered max on invoice (same unit). Aliases: `recorded_max_demand_kva`, `highest_actual_demand_kva`, etc.
- demand_charges: total $ for period (ex GST)
- meter_charges: period total of **all** metering-related lines (meter + VAS, etc.) for annualisation
- estimated_annual_usage_kwh: (total_usage_kwh / billing_days) * 365

GAS:
- MRIN: 8-12 characters (validate)
- total_usage_mj, total_usage_gj: extract (GJ = MJ / 1000)
- gas_rate_per_gj: ALWAYS extract or calculate (usage_charges / total_usage_gj)
- daily_supply_charge: in $/day
- estimated_annual_usage_gj: (total_usage_gj / billing_days) * 365

WASTE:
- waste_services: array with entry for each line item
  - service_type: string (e.g., "3M3 Frontlift General")
  - frequency: number (collections in period)
  - unit_cost: number
  - total_cost: number
  - pickup_dates: array of DD/MM/YYYY dates (extract or calculate)

WATER:
- volume_m3: number (convert to kL: m³ × 1000 for reporting)
- Calculate cost per kL for analysis

OIL:
- oil_services: array with entry for each line item

SAVINGS CALCULATION METHODOLOGY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Retail TOU (electricity):** §B comparison rates apply to **TOU** (bundled or **unbundled energy-only** c/kWh) — skip for flat/single-rate (see § WHEN POLICIES DO NOT APPLY).

**Daily supply:** **SME only** for savings messaging; **omit for C&I**. Gas: KB-driven as in guide.

**Metering:** Use § RETAIL & METERING POLICY tier comparisons (700 / 900).

**Demand:** § DEMAND REPRICING only when billed materially exceeds recorded max (see §); otherwise note alignment in prose without savings.

Annual projections:
- annual_usage = (period_usage / billing_days) * 365
- retail annual_savings (TOU) = (current_c/kWh - comparison_c/kWh) / 100 × annual_kWh for that TOU bucket

Minimum threshold:
- Only flag savings opportunities >$200/year

Final Estimates (for Excel workbook):
- Conservative: 70% of total calculated savings
- Moderate: 85% of total calculated savings
- Optimistic: 100% of total calculated savings
